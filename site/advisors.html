Robert Schriesheim

Adjunct Associate Professor of Finance, University of Chicago Booth School of Business

Rob Schriesheim is chairman of Truax Partners, where he advises boards, CEOs, and institutional investors on corporate transformations. He has served on 13 public company boards and as CFO of five public companies, including Sears Holdings and Hewitt Associates. He sits on the boards of Houlihan Lokey, Skyworks Solutions, and Alight, and teaches corporate governance at Chicago Booth.

Michael R. Levin

Activist Investor and Board Chair, Comarco, Inc.

Michael Levin is an activist investor and management consultant with almost thirty years of experience in investing, corporate finance, and equity turnarounds. He chairs the board of Comarco, Inc. and serves on the board of AG&E Holdings, Inc. He has held leadership positions at Towers Perrin, Deloitte, CNH, and Nicor, and holds bachelor’s and master’s degrees in economics from the University of Chicago.
