We train undergraduates to think, research, and act like activist investors.

What we do

  • Guest Speakers

    A speaker series featuring leading investors, advisors, executives, and other professionals who have shaped some of the most notable activist campaigns. Speakers share their perspectives on investing, corporate strategy, governance, and the decisions behind shareholder activism.

  • Educational Sessions

    A structured curriculum covering the foundations of investing and shareholder activism, from financial analysis and valuation to capital allocation, corporate governance, and activist strategy.

  • Activist Investment Pitches

    Members work in small groups to develop an activist thesis on a public company. Groups conduct fundamental research, evaluate the company’s strategy and operations, and lay out their recommendations for change, to be published each quarter.

  • Recruiting Bootcamp

    A preparation track for investment banking, private equity, and general finance recruiting. Sessions will include crash courses in accounting, valuation, modeling, and interview preparation, with mentorship and mock interviews from experienced members.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do we look for?

We hope to admit people who will embrace the challenge of activist investing and make the most of the club's opportunities. We look for genuine intellectual curiosity, intrinsic motivation, and strong business intuition. We are not looking for a previously established interest in, or knowledge of, investing.

What does the application process include?

A written application followed by interviews with our board members. We do not expect any prior knowledge or finance experience.

Do I need finance or activist investing experience to join?

No, and we encourage applicants without it. No prior finance, investing, or activist investing experience is required or expected. Our new member education assumes zero prior knowledge, and many of our most successful members entered the club with no background in finance.
